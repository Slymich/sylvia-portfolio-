FlyTrack Logistics

Enterprise Onboarding & Customer Success Plan

FlyTrack Logistics is a 1,200-person road-freight company operating 14 depots across Nigeria and Cameroon, signed to an ₦85 million annual contract. Their objective: a 30% reduction in delivery exceptions within six months — on the back of a previously failed ERP rollout that left leadership distrustful of new software. Every part of this onboarding framework is a direct response to six constraints surfaced at kick-off, not a generic SaaS template dropped onto a logistics company.

The constraints that shaped the framework

Six constraints surfaced at kick-off. Each one directly shaped a specific design decision — this wasn't a template, it was a response.

ConstraintWhy It MattersFramework Response
IT Manager: 4 hrs/week availableMore than 4 hrs/week would stall implementation — repeating the conditions of the previous ERP failure.CSM absorbs all configuration. IT involvement limited to SSO (2.5 hrs, one-time) and validation only.
14 geographically dispersed depotsSimultaneous rollout creates unmanageable support load and no learning loop between sites.Phased wave model: 2-depot pilot, then 6, then the remaining 6 — each wave informs the next.
Low digital literacy at depot levelGeneric training overwhelms basic-literacy supervisors, who revert to WhatsApp and phone calls within days.Role-segmented training; Hausa/English/French materials; laminated visual job aids; a Digital Champion per depot.
Failed ERP — prior 2-week shutdownStaff and leadership are primed to distrust new software; a big-bang go-live would trigger immediate resistance.Pilot-first approach so no single failure cascades. Visible quick win delivered to the Ops Director by Day 28.
CEO mandate: all 14 depots live in 90 daysAggressive timeline creates pressure to move fast without structure — repeating the ERP failure.Milestone gates between phases. Gates are signed off, not assumed.
Ops Director KPI: 30% exception reduction in 6 monthsOnboarding disconnected from this KPI loses executive sponsorship — the most common cause of Month 3-6 churn.Workflow Automation activated Phase 1, Day 10. Exception data collected from Day 14; baseline set by Day 45.

The phased wave rollout

14 depots, in three waves — not one big-bang launch.

Pilot

2 Depots

Days 10-12 go-live. Workflow Automation & Project Tracking live. 100% of pilot supervisors trained in English and Hausa.

Wave 1

6 Depots

Day 40 progressive go-live. Digital Champions active across all 8 depots so far. Under 15% data-entry error rate.

Wave 2

Remaining 6

Day 75 — all 14 depots live, peer-trained by Phase 1 Champions. 90% of all depot users trained by this point.
