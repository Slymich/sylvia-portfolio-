Obiageli Health Systems

MedCore 360 EHR — From Post-Incident Recovery to Renewal

A 31-site hospital group, fourteen months into a three-year contract. A platform update broke billing for four hours across all 31 sites — on their peak-billing Friday. ₦14.2M in revenue was delayed. A ₦2.5M credit was issued; the CMO accepted it but put in writing that it wasn't enough, and two competitors started calling her office. The insight that shaped the whole response: underneath the incident, the platform itself was excellent. This was a process failure, not a product failure — and a process can be fixed, visibly, for good.

The plan — ten months, three acts

Every assigned task sits inside one of three acts, in sequence.

Months 1-3

Recovery

Rebuild trust through total transparency. Fix the process, not just the code.

Months 4-6

Engagement

Turn critics into partners and prove value in money, not uptime.

Months 7-10

Growth

Capture an expansion that ties the vendor's success directly to theirs.

Act 1 · Recovery — the 60-day trust plan

Tailored to each stakeholder, in two phases: acknowledge first, then prove.

Days 1-30 · Acknowledge & StabiliseDays 31-60 · Prove & Re-engage
Post-incident report delivered by Day 10 (CSM + Engineering)First update issued under the new protocol — the proof point: zero incidents
Senior accountability call with the CMOExecutive Business Review — CMO co-signs the update, becoming a co-owner
In-person site visit to the flagship, most-exposed locationDeployment Lead installed as first-responder on site
Written sign-off rights and listening 1:1s for the billing teamContinued sign-off rights through the second phase
Three trust signals carry both phases: the post-incident report within ten days, the new protocol proven live on the next update, and an always-on client portal — transparency they can check any time, not promises they have to believe.

The change protocol — rewritten

From vendor-pushed to client-validated. Nothing above holds without this.

BeforeAfter
Updates pushed on vendor's schedule, including Fridays7 days' notice on every standard update, by email — never on a Friday
No formal testing gate before production72-hour UAT gate, tested in a sandbox, with written sign-off from billing + technical
Single-party deployment decisionDual sign-off required before anything touches production
No defined rollback process30-minute automatic rollback if anything breaks

Act 2 · Engagement — measuring sentiment honestly

Rather than asserting that relationships were repaired, sentiment was measured directly and on record — anonymous room sentiment tracked live at every review, giving leadership an honest trend line rather than a self-reported one.
