Wema Bank — Corporate Account Management

RICE-Model Prioritisation Under a Dual Crisis

Crestfield Agro-Allied Ltd. is a 5-year corporate banking client — ₦28M/year in fees and loan interest, active FX trade finance, payroll for 220 staff. Then two things hit in the same 48-hour window: three payroll runs had failed in four months, with staff complaining directly to the CEO, and Crestfield needed an urgent ₦180M expansion loan — while two competing banks were already in the room.

Reading the room, not just the spreadsheet, meant consciously elevating the loan proposal to the #1 strategic priority while executing the payroll fix immediately, in parallel.

Defend & repair — the payroll overhaul

Stop the bleeding before pitching the loan. A three-layer fix, defined before the client had to ask for one.

Layer 01 · Data

Pre-validation scrub

Payroll CSV files validated 24-48 hours before every disbursement — flagging formatting errors, duplicates, blank salaries, and invalid account numbers before they reach the system.

Layer 02 · Alert

Automated escalation

Any payroll batch sitting in "Pending" for more than 30 minutes triggers an automated alert cascade and pages the on-call systems engineer — zero discovery lag.

Layer 03 · Integrity

Proactive fee rebate

A 60-day corporate processing fee rebate applied to the account before it was requested. An operational failure became a demonstration of integrity instead.

The client doesn't wait to demand compensation — it's given before it's asked for. That's the difference between account management and relationship leadership.
